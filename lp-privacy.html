The problem with most finance apps

Every time you connect a bank account or sign in with your email, you're handing over your financial identity.

Your data on their servers

Most finance apps store your balances, transactions, and net worth on cloud servers you don't control. One breach exposes everything.

Bank connections = access

Connecting a bank account gives a third-party service read access to your transactions, balances, and account details. Every single one.

Invisible data harvesting

Analytics SDKs, ad networks, and "anonymous" usage data often include your financial behavior, spending patterns, and net worth range.

How Balance protects you

Four layers of privacy, built into the architecture — not bolted on as a feature.

01

100% offline

The app works without an internet connection. No data leaves your phone, ever. There's no server to hack because there's no server at all.

02

No bank connections

You enter your balances manually. This means no third-party service ever gets read access to your bank, brokerage, or exchange accounts.

03

No account, no login

Balance doesn't ask for your email, name, or any personal information. You download it, open it, and start using it. That's it.

04

Open source code

Every line of code is public. You — or anyone — can verify that Balance does exactly what it claims. No hidden analytics, no tracking pixels, no surprises.
